Mission Overview

The Artemis program aims to establish a sustainable human presence on the Moon by the end of the decade. Unlike the Apollo missions, Artemis is not just about landing — it is about building a long-term foundation for deep space exploration.

Through advanced spacecraft, international collaboration, and new lunar technologies, NASA will prepare humanity for future missions to Mars.

Core Objectives

Lunar Sustainability

Develop systems to maintain human life on the Moon for extended periods.

Gateway Station

Build a lunar orbital outpost to support missions deeper into space.

Mars Preparation

Test technologies and systems required for future Mars exploration.

Artemis II Crew

Artemis II will be the first crewed mission of the program, sending astronauts around the Moon in preparation for future landings.
